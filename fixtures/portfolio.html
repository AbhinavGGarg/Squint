The short version

I started Falcon Hacks so people at my school could build a project and then present it to a room.

What it is

It is a hackathon club. Students come to build things and show them. I run the meetings, help people brainstorm, show them Python and AI tools, and coach the demos.

What I actually do

Run meetingsClub meetings where students bring ideas and leave with a plan.
Brainstorm with studentsHelp turn a vague 'it would be cool if' into a project small enough to finish.
Teach the toolsIntroduce Python and AI tools to students who haven't built with them before.
Coach demosPractice presenting out loud, more than once, in front of people.
Plan the hackathonScheduling, the club website, and outreach for the club's first event.
