Invoices that chase themselves.

Ledger sends the invoice, nudges the client on day 15 and day 30, and matches the payment to your bank feed when it lands. For studios of two to twenty people.

Start with one client

Two things it does that your spreadsheet doesn't

Both were built after watching a design studio in Leeds spend a Friday afternoon on money that was already owed to them.

Reminders with a tone you chose

Day 15 is friendly, day 30 is firm, day 45 copies the person who signed the contract. You write each one once; Ledger fills in the amount, the invoice number and the payment link.

Reconciliation that reads the reference

When £4,200.00 arrives with "INV-0931" in the reference, that invoice is marked paid and the client's reminder sequence stops. Partial payments get a line, not a guess. See how bank feeds connect.

Send an invoice from a script

Everything in the app is on the API. This is the whole call.

  -u sk_live_…: \
  -d customer=cus_8Hn2 \
  -d "reply_to=accounts@example.com" \
  -d amount=420000 -d currency=gbp
